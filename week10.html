Week of October 26, 2026

Week 10

This week I continued the research I started last week on social enterprise financing in developing countries. I used NotebookLM to work through the papers and ChatGPT to help me synthesize what I was finding.

Nothing explicitly went wrong this week. What I did have to do was push back on ChatGPT to narrow the synthesis so that it actually reflected my own findings rather than a broader version of them. That is the same thing I had to do with NotebookLM last week. Both tools default to giving me something wide, and the narrowing is the part I have to do myself. The difference this week was that I expected it, so I caught it faster.

I am also starting to think about changes to our MVP based on this research. The main one is making the onboarding questions more exhaustive. Right now they do not capture enough of the financing context that the AI would need in order for its recommendations to be grounded in a user's actual circumstances. This connects back to our Week 6 prototype and the peer feedback we got in Week 7, which both pointed to the onboarding being too general. The research has given me a clearer sense of what specifically is missing, rather than just that something was.

AI tools this week: NotebookLM — continued analyzing research papers on social enterprise financing. ChatGPT — synthesized findings from that research.
