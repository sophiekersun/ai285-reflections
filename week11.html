Week of November 2, 2026

Week 11

This week I used ChatGPT for three different things.

First, I used it to work through the assignment due this week. Instead of giving it everything at once, I walked it through step by step and guided it through each of the updates we were making. This is different from how I was using these tools earlier in the semester. In Week 7 I wrote about ChatGPT wanting to make too many changes at once. Going step by step and keeping control of the order has made that much less of a problem.

Second, I continued using it for my research on social enterprise financing.

Third, I used it to build a time plan for the next few weeks so that Andy and I stay focused on what we need to get done for the MVP. This was the first time I have used AI to plan the work itself rather than to produce something.

The time plan it gave me was too packed. It assumed we could get through more in a week than is realistic alongside everything else we have going on, so I spread the work out over a longer period. It was a useful starting point, but the pace it set was not one we could actually hold.

AI tools this week: ChatGPT — worked through this week's assignment step by step, continued my research on social enterprise financing, and built a time plan for the MVP work over the next few weeks. What I changed: the time plan it created was too packed, so I spread the work out over a longer period to make it realistic.
